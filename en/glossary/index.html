Glossary

Mẽbêngôkre words

Words in the Mẽbêngôkre language and acronyms used on this site, with their meaning and source.

Mẽbêngôkre
The people’s own name: “people of the water hole”. Also the name of their language, the same spoken by the Kayapó.Source: Xikrin Life Plan
Kukradjà
The Xikrin way of being and living: living with the forest, together with the forest and for the forest.Source: Xikrin PGTA
Kubengêt
The elders, “those who truly know”. The council of elders and chiefs is the highest collective decision-making body.Source: Xikrin Life Plan
Menire
Xikrin women.Source: Annex 1 of the Restaura Amazônia project
Meprire
Childhood. “Since meprire”: since early childhood.Source: Annex 1 of the Restaura Amazônia project
Ngàb
The warriors’ house, the political space for decisions in each village.Source: Xikrin Life Plan
Aben Kaben Marimei
Name of the Xikrin Territorial and Environmental Management Plan: “knowledge shared by all”.Source: Annex 1 of the Restaura Amazônia project
Kwyrykangô
A Xikrin ritual and cultural festival.Source: Berê Network Institutional Portfolio
Kuben
Non-Indigenous people.Source: Former Association website
TI (Terra Indígena)
Indigenous Territory: land officially recognized by the Brazilian state for the exclusive use of an Indigenous people.Source: Brazilian Federal Constitution
SAF
Agroforestry system (sistema agroflorestal): native trees and crops grown together, like a forest that produces.Source: Restoration Plan
CLPI
Free, prior and informed consent (FPIC) of the community.Source: Annex 1 of the Restaura Amazônia project
PGTA
Territorial and Environmental Management Plan.Source: Xikrin PGTA
PBA-CI
Indigenous Component of the Basic Environmental Plan of the Belo Monte dam: the compensation and mitigation program for Indigenous peoples.Source: Former Association website

Spellings follow the cited sources. Standardization of written Mẽbêngôkre is carried out by the Association, with Kroire Xikrin.
