Support

Strengthen those who protect the forest

Support strengthens territorial protection, rituals and income from the forest, with decisions in the hands of the villages.

Where support goes

What your support strengthens

  • Surveillance and protection of the Trincheira Bacajá Indigenous Territory
  • Rituals and the passing of knowledge between generations
  • Income from forest products and menire handicrafts
  • Communication and mobility between the 16 villages
